THE FINDING IN ONE SENTENCE:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Swiss workers in firms adopting AI face real wage losses (8.5% overall, 17.6% for 
women), triggering coherent political realignment toward left-wing ideology and 
demand for labor protection policies.

THE STORY IN THREE PARTS:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PART 1: WHAT WE OBSERVE
  • Firms adopting AI show 8.5% wage loss for exposed workers (FY-FE)
  • Losses concentrated on women (17.6%) and older workers (15.8%)
  • Political response: Males shift left (-0.43**), females don't (-0.01)
  • Job insecurity partially explains political shift (partial mediation)

PART 2: WHAT IT MEANS
  • Mechanism: Firm-level labor market disruption with transparent attribution
  • Identification: Moderate-high confidence (FY-FE appropriate, selection untested)
  • Selection bias: ≈0.1935 (why OY-FE gives opposite sign)
  • Remaining confounders: Selection on unobservables, reverse causality

PART 3: WHY IT MATTERS
  • Political economy: Economic threat → political demand for state protection
  • Gender gap: Male response rational (breadwinners); female non-response 
    reflects lower political efficacy
  • Policy window: 2026-2027 (before cumulative losses intensify polarization)
  • Solutions: Wage insurance + impact assessments + collective bargaining

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RESOLVING THE INCOME REVERSAL:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FY-FE:  -8.5% wage LOSS   ← Within-firm causal effect (CORRECT for wages)
OY-FE: +10.9% wage GAIN   ← Between-firm selection effect (CORRECT for selection)

NOT a contradiction. They identify different phenomena:
  • FY-FE: When my firm adopts AI → I lose wages
  • OY-FE: High-wage firms (which adopt AI) employ more workers

Selection bias = 0.1935 (≈60% of OY-FE effect is selection, not causation)

PHASE 1 ROBUSTNESS TESTS (2 WEEKS):
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TEST 1: Pre-Trend Analysis (1 week)
  Question: Do future-exposed workers earn less BEFORE AI adoption?
  Expected if causal: No (trends should be parallel pre-treatment)
  Expected if selection: Yes (wage loss appears before treatment date)

TEST 2: Placebo Test (1 week)
  Question: If we assign fake exposure 1-2 years before actual, does wage loss appear?
  Expected if causal: No (should be null)
  Expected if selection: Yes (wage differences pre-date real treatment)

TEST 3: Lead/Lag Specification (1 week)
  Question: Does future exposure (t+1) predict past politics (t)?
  Expected if causal: No (no leads should be significant)
  Expected if reverse causality: Yes (leads significant means reverse effect)
