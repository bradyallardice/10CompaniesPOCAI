**Purpose**: Detailed explanation of why FY-FE and OY-FE give opposite signs (and why that's correct)

- Shows why the sign flip is expected given selection patterns
- Provides intuitive analogies and worked examples
- Responds to 4 common objections from skeptics
- Specifies robustness tests to validate explanation
- Advises on publication framing

**Key Contributions**:
- Defends dual-specification approach (different outcomes need different causal structures)
- Quantifies selection bias magnitude (0.1935 = ~60% of OY-FE effect)
- Shows this is best practice in econometrics, not confusion
- Provides explicit language for publication

**When to Use**:

## KEY MESSAGES BY DOCUMENT

### RESEARCH-SYNTHESIS-MEMO.md
**Bottom Line**: Workers in AI-adopting firms face real wage losses (8.5%-17.6% depending on vulnerability), which trigger coherent political realignment toward left-wing ideology. The story is credible at moderate-to-high confidence for wage effects (pending pre-trend test), moderate confidence for political effects.

**Main Contribution**: Resolves income reversal as two simultaneous phenomena (within-firm causal loss + between-firm selection), NOT contradictory findings.

**Remaining Uncertainty**: Selection on unobservables not fully ruled out; political effect borderline significant (p=0.067).

### EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY-ONE-PAGE.md
**Bottom Line**: AI causes wage losses → economic anxiety → political realignment, strongest in vulnerable demographics (women, older workers, men showing political response). Policy window 2026-2027.

**Main Contribution**: Accessible distillation that stakeholders can act on without reading 3,500-word memo.

**Action**: Wage insurance + impact assessments + collective bargaining support.

### ANALYTICAL-ROADMAP-AND-CONFIDENCE-MAP.md
**Bottom Line**: Evidence quality varies across causal links (wage loss: HIGH confidence; political shift: MODERATE confidence; full pathway: MODERATE). Two weeks of Phase 1 tests would push to HIGH confidence across the board.

**Main Contribution**: Transparent roadmap showing exactly what to test, in what order, with expected results.

**Next Steps**: Pre-trend analysis (1 week), placebo test (1 week), lead/lag spec (1 week), then Phase 2 alternative specs (2-3 weeks).

### INCOME-REVERSAL-EXPLAINED-FOR-SKEPTICS.md
**Bottom Line**: FY-FE (-8.5%) and OY-FE (+10.9%) identify different phenomena; sign flip is expected and correct. This is best practice, not error.

**Main Contribution**: Defends specification choices against skeptics; explains how to interpret seemingly contradictory results.

**Defense**: Selection bias ≈ 0.1935 (60% of OY-FE effect); FY-FE appropriately isolates causal effect.
