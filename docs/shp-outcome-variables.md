## Flags for Empirical Design

**Strong panel candidates** (full or near-full coverage):
- `P$$P10` (left-right), `P$$P19` (vote intention) are the only political variables with continuous annual coverage — strongest candidates for panel DiD or FE models.
- `P$$C17` (depression/anxiety), `P$$C44` (life satisfaction, from 2000), `P$$W101` (unemployment risk), `P$$E18` (training), and all income/hours variables work well for panel estimation.

**Job security**: `P$$W86` (1999–2003) and `P$$W86A` (2004–2023) use different wording. Needs harmonization before use in a pooled panel.

**"Rotating battery" political variables** (`P$$P02`, `P03`, `P04`, `P13`, `P14`, `P15`, `P17`): Asked every year 1999–2009, then only in 2011/2014/2017/2020/2023. Missing in 2010, 2012, 2013, 2015, 2016, 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022. Use with caution in panel FE; better suited for repeated cross-sections.

**One-time or sparse modules** (not suitable for panel analysis):
- Perceived stress `C180–C183`: 2016 only
- Financial worries `C112–C114`: 2012 only
- SVP sympathy `P72`, spending preferences `P61`/`P63`: 2011, 2014, 2017 only
- Anomie `P87–P93`: 2014, 2018, 2021 only
- Burnout/depression diagnoses `C205–C207`: 2021–2023 only
